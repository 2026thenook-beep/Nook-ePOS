# The Nook ePOS 3.9.10 — Streamlined Payment Workflow

- Replaced the permanent Cash, Card, Cash Paid and Change controls on the Till with one large PAY button.
- PAY opens a simple Cash or Card selector. No mixed-payment option is included.
- Cash payment now shows Amount Due, Cash Received and a live prominent CHANGE / STILL TO PAY panel.
- Complete Sale remains disabled until the cash received covers the ticket total.
- Added Exact amount shortcut and retained the automatic-pence keypad behaviour.
- Customer name and table number remain available within the cash payment screen.
- Card payment retains the confirmation screen before committing the transaction.
- Transactions are only cleared after the existing server-confirmed save completes.
- Cash receipt summaries show Cash Received and a larger, bold Change value.
- Hold and Clear remain visible beside the new PAY action, with a stacked responsive layout on small screens.
